# Tonkean

> Tonkean is an enterprise AI process-orchestration platform ("the AI front door for internal service teams"). Procurement, legal, and IT/HR service teams use its no-code process builder, AI agents, and 100+ data-source integrations to orchestrate intake, triage, and fulfillment across existing enterprise systems. Tonkean does not publish a public developer API; ingress is via webhook connections and no-code custom data sources.
